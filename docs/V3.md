# V3: Reaction clips

Short real-life moments are turned into **reaction** clips (format `"reaction"`, type `"Reaction"`): screams and shouts, jumps, dives, and round-end celebrations.
Code lives in `src/Composer/Reactions.cs` (pure detectors), `ReactionJob.cs` and the worker. It runs in the Capture service like V2.

## Detection (per match / session)
- **Room audio** (camera mic, from the Capture service):
  - Loudness is the RMS of 100 ms windows with a 50 ms hop, measured against the session median.
  - A **peak** is louder than the median + 15 dB (`ThresholdDb`) for ≥ 250 ms. Peaks closer than 3 s are merged.
  - Short clicks and bangs are ignored.
  - No laughter/scream classification.
- **Body** comes from all round demos of the match. Unity keeps the session's demos (`KeepSessionDemos`, newest 40), and only v3 demos with a UTC anchor are used.
  - **Jump:** the head rises ≥ 0.25 m above the standing baseline (the median head height of the round) within 0.4 s, then comes back down.
  - **Dive / deep crouch:** the head drops ≥ 0.6 m in < 0.5 s.
  - **Celebration:** within 5 s after RoundEnd, ≥ 2 players of the winning team have a hand above their head.
- **Scoring:**

| Event | Score |
|---|---|
| Audio peak | 10 + 2·(dB above median − 15) + 5·length (max 3 s) |
| Celebration | 25 + 5 per extra player |
| Jump | 12 + 10·(height − 0.25), +4 per extra simultaneous jumper |
| Dive | 10 |

- Events within 3 s of each other form one reaction. The peak is the loudest audio moment, otherwise the strongest body event.
- Reactions are chosen best first, with a **20 s cooldown** and **max 3 per session** (`ReactionOptions`).
- **Involved players:** the jumpers/divers/celebrators. For audio-only peaks it is the player nearest to the camera (mic) at that moment.
- **Hero:** the single involved player, or `none` when several players are involved (centroid track).

## Clip
- **Window:** 4 s before to 4 s after the peak.
- **Layers:** Unity `-renderlayers <demo> <reactionId> -hero <clientId|none> -overlay light …` (light overlay: rings, markers, banners; no POV with `-hero none`).
  - Without a demo or calibration for that moment, the clip is plain footage with a transparent overlay.
- **Edit (`ReactionEdl`):**
  - no cold open;
  - 3.5 s lead-in at 1x;
  - the peak at 0.6x with flash and zoom punch;
  - 2.5 s after the peak at 1x;
  - the 1.2 s end card.
  - Total length is 6–10 s, usually 8.2 s.
- **Text:**
  - hook per kind: "When it gets real." (scream), "Round won. Real legs." (celebration), "Jumped. In real life.", "Hit the deck. For real.";
  - REAL VR badge, VRLink watermark;
  - no kill counter.
- **Audio:** room audio prominent, game −10 dB, the same ducking, −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP.
- **Contract:**
  - `highlightId` is `<match8>-rx<N>`, with `matchId` and `round`;
  - `heroIsBot` is true when all involved players are bots;
  - `players[]` lists the involved labels;
  - `sources` are `arenaCam`, plus `overlay`/`gameAudio` when there is a layer pack, plus `roomAudio`.

## Session pin
While `server_state.json` reports `matchRunning`, the worker keeps a rolling pin from the match start to now + 3 min, refreshed every minute. This means a long match is still fully available after the 15 min retention. The pin is released after the match's reactions are done.

## Run
- **Automatic:** the Capture service with `Composer:Enabled` and `Composer:ReactionsEnabled` (default true). Runs once per match, after its last demo is > 2 min old and the gate is open.
- **Manual:**
  - `vrlink-compose reactions --match <matchId>` lists the detected reactions;
  - add `--compose [--force]` to render them;
  - add `--fake-layers` to skip Unity.

## Proof
- **Unit tests:**
  - synthetic room audio: two screams 2 s apart merge into one peak, a 120 ms click is ignored;
  - synthetic poses: jump, dive and celebration are found with the right players and UTC; standing sway is not;
  - planner: cooldown, max 3, merging, nearest player;
  - reaction edit: 6–10 s, no cold open, punch on the peak, no counter.
- **End-to-end** (`ReactionEndToEndTests`): TEST camera source + v3 demo with a jump → `abcdef12-rx1.mp4`
  - 1080×1920, 30 fps, 8.233 s, −14.0 LUFS, true peak −6.4 dBFS;
  - previews: `v3/v3_reaction_*.png`.
  - The TEST source's 100 ms beeps are correctly not counted as peaks (below 250 ms).
